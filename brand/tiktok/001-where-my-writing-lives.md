# TikTok 001: Where my writing lives now

**Goal:** tell your TikTok followers the full story now lives on Substack, and that facebook.com/sarahnotplain is the real you.
**Length:** about 30 seconds. **Format:** 9:16 vertical.

## Script (your voice; change any words so they sound like you)

| Time | You say | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | "For over a year I've been telling you my story in pieces." | **You, to camera.** Text: *the long version is here* |
| 3–10s | "I'm writing all of it now. As a book." | **Clip A**: your hands with real photos, or your notebook/laptop |
| 10–18s | "I'm publishing it chapter by chapter on Substack, as I write it. It's called Not Plain." | **Clip B**: the "There, but not here" quote card |
| 18–25s | "It's the parts that never fit in sixty seconds. My dad. My family. What I remember, and what I only know from photographs." | **Clip C**: the "Secrets started my life" print card |
| 25–30s | "The link's in my bio. And if you find me on Facebook, the real page is Sarah Not Plain. That's the only one that's me." | **End card** (`brand/tiktok/end-card.png`) |

Hook tip: the first 2 seconds decide whether people stay, so film the opening line looking straight into the camera. If you'd rather not be on camera, use Clip A with the text *"I'm writing the whole story now"* and voice-over only.

## Visuals (no AI)

- **Clip A:** film it yourself. Your hands going through a few real photos (backs, edges or shoulders only, if you'd rather not show faces), or you writing. Five seconds, natural light.
- **Clips B and C:** your branded quote cards: `posts/there-but-not-here/pinterest.png` and `posts/secrets-five-days-before-christmas/pinterest.png` (tall versions that fit a phone screen). In TikTok or CapCut, add each as a photo and apply a slow zoom effect.
- **End card:** `brand/tiktok/end-card.png`.

Or keep it simple: film the whole thing talking to camera. For your audience, that may do best of all.

## Putting it together (in TikTok or CapCut)

1. Film the opening line (or record the whole script as voice-over).
2. Add Clips A, B, C in order, then the end card for the last 4 to 5 seconds.
3. Turn on auto-captions. Most people watch without sound.
4. Caption and hashtags below.

## Caption

> I'm writing the whole story now, as a book, and publishing it chapter by chapter on Substack. It's called Not Plain. Link in bio.
>
> #memoir #booktok #writingcommunity #substack #grief

## Before posting

Set your TikTok bio link to your Substack with a tracking tag, so Substack shows TikTok as the source:

```
https://sarahnotplain.substack.com/?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bio
```
