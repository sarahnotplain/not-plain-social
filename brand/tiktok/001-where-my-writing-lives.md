# TikTok 001: Where my writing lives now

**Goal:** tell your TikTok followers the full story now lives on Substack, and that facebook.com/sarahnotplain is the real you.
**Length:** about 30 seconds. **Format:** 9:16 vertical.

## Script (your voice; change any words so they sound like you)

| Time | You say | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | "For over a year I've been telling you my story in pieces." | **You, to camera.** Text: *the long version is here* |
| 3–10s | "I'm writing all of it now. As a book." | **Clip A**: old photos |
| 10–18s | "I'm publishing it chapter by chapter on Substack, as I write it. It's called Not Plain." | **Clip B**: dirt road from the backseat |
| 18–25s | "It's the parts that never fit in sixty seconds. My dad. My family. What I remember, and what I only know from photographs." | **Clip C**: Christmas tree, 1990 |
| 25–30s | "The link's in my bio. And if you find me on Facebook, the real page is Sarah Not Plain. That's the only one that's me." | **End card** (`brand/tiktok/end-card.png`) |

Hook tip: the first 2 seconds decide whether people stay, so film the opening line looking straight into the camera. If you'd rather not be on camera, use Clip A with the text *"I'm writing the whole story now"* and voice-over only.

## Higgsfield clips (make each one 5 seconds, vertical 9:16)

Rules for every clip: no faces, no people you could recognize, nothing violent or crime-related. The mood is quiet and remembered, not dramatic.

**Clip A: old photos**
> Close-up of a woman's hands slowly turning over a stack of faded 1990s 5x7 family photographs on a wooden table, warm lamp light, only the backs of the photos and edges visible, no faces, shallow depth of field, 35mm film grain, soft slow push-in, quiet and nostalgic, vertical 9:16

**Clip B: the dirt road**
> View from the back seat of a 1990s forest green car driving slowly down a narrow Texas dirt road on a hot summer afternoon, blackberry bushes and overgrown trees on both sides, dust in golden sunlight, seen through a slightly dusty side window, handheld home-video feel, 35mm film grain, no people visible, vertical 9:16

**Clip C: Christmas, 1990**
> A dim 1990 living room at night lit mostly by a Christmas tree sprayed with fake snow and hung with antique ornaments, an ornate hand-carved wooden mantel above an unlit fireplace, no people, slow gentle camera drift toward the tree, warm glow, VHS-era film grain, quiet and nostalgic, vertical 9:16

If a clip comes out with faces or looks too glossy or cinematic, add *"muted colors, amateur home video, no people"* and generate it again.

## Putting it together (in TikTok or CapCut)

1. Film the opening line (or record the whole script as voice-over).
2. Add Clips A, B, C in order, then the end card for the last 4 to 5 seconds.
3. Turn on auto-captions. Most people watch without sound.
4. Label it: TikTok → **More options → AI-generated content** on, because the clips are AI-made.
5. Caption and hashtags below.
