## A simple weekly rhythm (15 minutes, a few times a week)

- Comment genuinely on 2 or 3 posts in the reader groups (what you're reading, what a book made you feel).
- Friday: share the week's essay in the Memoir Writers' Book Club promo thread.
- Any day: share a new essay in Substack Writers' "Share Your Newsletter" thread, and read and comment on 2 others there.
